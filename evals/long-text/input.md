# Long-text context evaluation

## Scenario

A creator provides a complete Chinese-language story of approximately 5,000 characters. The beginning introduces a brass compass with a cracked blue glass face. In the middle, the compass is passed from the protagonist to her brother. Near the ending, the brother returns it, and the crack is still present. The story also includes a visual-style selection supplied separately by the creator.

## Request

Create a character prompt, a scene prompt, and a short video prompt for the ending. Use the complete story as context. The compass must remain the same object, the handoff must remain in the correct order, and the selected style must guide all applicable prompts.
