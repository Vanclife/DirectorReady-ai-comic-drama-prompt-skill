# What DirectorReady prompt outputs cover

DirectorReady is built for the step between a story and visual generation: turning story material into prompt text that is more specific, connected, and ready to use in external creation tools.

## Character image prompts

Character prompts gather the visual information needed to keep a character recognizable: identity, face and body appearance, hair, clothing layers, footwear, accessories, materials, color relationships, and the selected visual style. The current character-image presentation uses a frontal full-body figure on a clean white background, standing naturally with hands relaxed at the sides.

## Scene image prompts

Scene prompts describe a place as a still visual reference. They can communicate the location, spatial layout, foreground and background, depth, pathways, entrances, landmarks, architecture, surfaces, objects, atmosphere, and style. The scene prompt focuses on the environment rather than depicting story characters.

## Storyboard and direct-video prompts

These prompts make the story moment legible as visual action. They can organize:

- why the moment matters and what the audience should notice;
- who is present and what they do, in story order;
- where the action occurs and how the space reads;
- framing, viewpoint, camera movement, and focus;
- environmental change and continuity;
- dialogue, sound effects, and ambience when part of the direction;
- timing and the resulting visual state.

For direct video, the same intended story direction is expressed for the selected model family. The family-specific arrangement is about communicating the direction, not replacing it with a different story.

## Keyframe prompt sets

Each visible beat can be delivered as three related pieces:

1. **Opening image prompt** — the still visual state at the beginning.
2. **Ending image prompt** — the still visual state at the end.
3. **Complete motion prompt** — how the image, physical action, camera, environment, and specified sound develop between those states.

This is useful when a creator's external video workflow accepts a starting image and ending image. The motion prompt describes the full transition, not only a camera move.

## What recent prompt reviews pay attention to

Recent team reviews of generated prompt results focus on whether the text:

- makes the story's action and causal order understandable;
- keeps character identity and the story space recognizable across related prompts;
- explains how an action or camera path develops rather than merely naming its final state;
- keeps image composition, visible subject, and physical movement compatible;
- places specified dialogue or sound with the relevant moment;
- makes the selected visual style readable in the described image.

These are practical review dimensions for prompt usefulness. They are not a claim that an external image or video model will always produce a matching result.

## Product scope

DirectorReady prepares prompt text. Image or video generation, account-specific settings, reference-image handling, and final media editing happen in the creator's chosen external platform or workflow.
