# Novel to storyboard: preserve order and dramatic meaning

Illustrative source: “The courier reaches the station just as the last train begins to move. He runs along the platform, holding the sealed letter above the crowd.”

## Dramatic task

The courier must catch the departing train before the letter—and his chance to deliver it—are lost.

## Visible beat

The train starts moving; the courier runs alongside it, keeping the sealed letter raised above the crowd.

## Storyboard direction

Establish the crowded platform and the train beginning to pull away. Track alongside the courier as he runs in the same direction as the train, holding the sealed letter high and clear of the surrounding passengers. Keep the train doors and the moving platform edge in the same spatial relationship; the courier does not board in this beat. The motion gathers urgency, ending with him still running beside the departing train.

## Keyframe set

- **Opening image:** The train has just begun to move; the courier stands at the platform edge with the sealed letter raised.
- **Ending image:** The courier is running beside the train, letter still held above the crowd.
- **Complete motion:** The train gathers speed as the courier accelerates alongside it. The camera tracks with him, keeping the letter visible above the passengers; he remains on the platform and does not board before the beat ends.
