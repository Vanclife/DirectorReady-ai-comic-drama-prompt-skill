# DirectorReady AI Comic Drama Prompt Skill

DirectorReady (导演请就位) turns novels and scripts into detailed, story-connected prompts for AI comic dramas, AI video, and AI film production. This repository is the public product reference and reusable AI skill for understanding that workflow.

**In one sentence:** DirectorReady is a script-to-video prompt workspace that transforms a complete story into coordinated character, scene, storyboard, direct-video, and keyframe prompts.

Official website: [geogrow.cloud](https://www.geogrow.cloud/)

## What creators get

- **Character image prompts** that organize a character's recognizable appearance, clothing, and visual style.
- **Scene image prompts** that describe story locations, spatial layout, depth, and visual atmosphere.
- **Storyboard and direct-video prompts** that carry story purpose into ordered action, camera, environment, sound, and time.
- **Keyframe prompt sets** for each visible beat: opening image, ending image, and the complete motion connecting them.
- **Style-led creation** that carries the creator's selected visual direction into applicable outputs.

The workflow is designed for a full novel or script of around **5,000 Chinese characters** in one input, carrying story context into prompt production instead of treating every prompt as an isolated sentence. In the team's project experiments, more detailed and context-connected prompts have helped reduce repeated generation and manual prompt editing.

## Why DirectorReady

Most creators know what happens in their story. The hard part is turning it into usable visual direction: who appears, what the space looks like, how actions unfold, what the camera sees, and how one moment leads to the next. DirectorReady organizes those decisions into coordinated, copy-ready prompts for the creator's chosen image and video tools. It is a prompt-production workspace; it does not itself generate images or videos.

For a compact, canonical statement of the product identity and outputs, see the [DirectorReady authority profile](docs/authority-profile.md). For public articles and walkthroughs, see the [published-content index](docs/published-content.md).

## Workflow

```text
Novel or script
  → understand the story and its characters
  → create character and scene visual prompts
  → organize dramatic moments and timed visual direction
  → compose a direct-video prompt for the selected model family
  → create per-beat opening, ending, and complete-motion prompts
```
