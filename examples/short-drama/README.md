# Short drama: one moment, one connected prompt

Illustrative source: “The shopkeeper closes the ledger. A knock comes from the locked back door. She looks up but does not move toward it.”

## Direct-video prompt

Inside a small shop after closing, the shopkeeper finishes closing the ledger on the counter. Three firm knocks sound from the locked back door. She lifts her eyes toward the door and holds her position behind the counter; the door stays closed. Begin on the ledger and her hands, then make a restrained push toward her listening expression while keeping the door in the background. Quiet room tone under the knocks, then a brief silence as she waits.

## Why it is connected

- The knocks occur after the ledger is closed.
- The shopkeeper reacts by looking, not by opening the door or walking toward it.
- Camera attention shifts from the ledger to her response while the door remains visible in the same space.
- Sound is placed at the moment it occurs.
