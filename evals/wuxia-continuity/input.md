# Wuxia continuity evaluation

## Source

At dusk, Shen Yu reaches the old stone bridge. A wounded guard is waiting beneath the eastern arch. Shen Yu steps off the bridge onto the riverbank, circles around a fallen cart, and stops between the guard and the narrow path to the village. The guard raises his lantern; its light reveals a red seal on Shen Yu's sleeve. Shen Yu does not draw his sword.

## Request

Create a short storyboard/direct-video prompt that makes the movement and spatial relationships easy to follow. Keep the bridge, eastern arch, riverbank, fallen cart, village path, lantern, and red seal in their stated positions and sequence.
